> *"I build digital products that turn complex real-world workflows into reliable, scalable software."*

I am an Ethiopian software engineer, systems architect, and founder with **9+ years of professional experience** spanning system implementation, corporate training, quality assurance, and end-to-end full-stack software development at **PEDS** and **Sera X**.

- 🔭 **Founder @ Sera X Technologies**: Building an integrated ecosystem of enterprise software across ERP, retail POS, healthcare, logistics, and practical AI.
- 🎓 **BSc in Computer Science** — University of Gondar *(Class of 2022)*.
- ⚖️ **Bachelor of Laws (LL.B)** — Year 1 *(Bridging software architecture with digital policy, regulatory compliance, IP, and legal tech)*.
- 🏥 **Social Impact**: Volunteered to deploy and customize a complete Hospital ERP system for a public village health clinic in Ethiopia.
- 💼 **Full Stack Software Engineer** at **PEDS** (2017 – Present).
